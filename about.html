    My Story

    From Personal Discovery
    to Professional Purpose

    My journey into homeopathy began not in a classroom, but at home — as a mother searching for gentle, effective solutions for my family. When conventional medicine left us with more questions than answers, a trusted friend introduced us to homeopathy. The results were life-changing.

    That personal transformation ignited a passion I could not ignore. I pursued rigorous training in classical homeopathy, learning to see each person as a unique individual rather than a collection of symptoms. I believe deeply that true healing honours the whole person — body, mind, and spirit.

    Today, I am proud to serve families across Calgary and the surrounding communities of Alberta. My practice is built on the values of patience, deep listening, and genuine care.
